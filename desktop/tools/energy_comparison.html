Equations and how to interpret the comparison

Rhythm combines 60% attack frequency and 40% spectral movement. Fullness combines 50% separated-part participation, 25% bass activity and 25% occupied spectrum. The exact shares are experiments, not fitted research coefficients. A larger plotted swing can also come from each curve's percentile scaling. The section table includes the change before that scaling.

Internal-change rankings exclude the first four and final eight seconds, plus the transition into a detected quiet ending. The ending remains visible in the graph.
